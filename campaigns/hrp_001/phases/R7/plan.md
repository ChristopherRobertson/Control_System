## Phase-specific procedure and deliverables

This phase inherits the campaign-wide scientific, safety, acquisition, analysis, and data-contract requirements in `../../requirements.md`.

| R7 — MVP acquisition | ≥3 independent preparations, randomized/interleaved blocks | Per-block QC and completion of planned controls |

The room-temperature MVP uses the R5 slow-scan anchors and R6 reversible envelope. It
must name its frozen configuration-specific IRF, HF2LI settings, native coverage,
recovery/reset rule, scan trajectory, phase schedule, and reconstruction version. If
repeated rapid-scan phase-delay acquisition is retained, validate forward/reverse,
filter memory, missing scans/samples, heteroscedastic noise, interpolation support,
edge effects, and identifiable regions and retain the fixed-wavelength recovery record
as a distinct architecture. Do not pool methods or invent unsupported coverage.
