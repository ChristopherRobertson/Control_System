# Instrument-readiness promotion workspace

Promotion review material belongs here. Runtime products are copied to
`instrument/promoted_bundles/` only after their scientific closeout gates and the
required explicit promotion authorization are satisfied.

A promotion review also requires an accepted `procedural_writeup.md` for every
source phase material to the proposed value, correction, uncertainty, validity
envelope, or claim. The writeups provide the human-readable reasoning; indexed
machine-readable evidence remains the numerical authority. Missing historical
writeups are backfilled from existing evidence and never justify reacquisition by
themselves.
