# Frozen exclusions

- Broad 1650–2050 cm^-1 probe survey or any grid beyond the local
  Mylar/polystyrene carbonyl window and combined 1885–1980 cm^-1 biological
  region.
- Direct 1064 nm sample-path characterization; residual 1064 nm is only a
  source-health/separation/safety observation.
- 355 nm as a sample pump; it is retained solely as the OPO drive.
- Broad OPO tuning or wavelengths other than the retained 540 nm output.
- Direct pulse-energy distributions, pulse-to-pulse energy jitter, or
  calibrated peak-power claims.
- Optional mechanistic, quantum-yield, 532-vs-540 comparison, additional
  biological band/state, concentration-series, flow-cell, or translation-stage
  extensions.
- Biological samples, proteins, CO, dithionite, biological controls, sample
  preparation, state verification, sample-specific dose/damage/recovery, or
  executable biological recipes.
- Optional hardware, metrology resources, certificates, identities, points,
  repetitions, or calendar-driven work not required by the frozen union.
