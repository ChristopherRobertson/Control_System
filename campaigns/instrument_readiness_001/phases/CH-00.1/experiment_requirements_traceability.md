## Consolidated unresolved gaps blocking recipe freeze or thesis claims

1. No calibration or characterization bundle is promoted; every measured-value
   import and final configuration remains unavailable.
2. `MS-02.1` has not established the installed dual-recorder branch transfer,
   MIRcat Sweep Active-to-PicoScope-EXT trigger route, or complete PicoScope operating
   uncertainty; MSW-01 has not established the cross-recorder Sweep Active relation.
3. `WM-01` is deferred until a suitable replacement spectrometer is qualified;
   independent 540 nm center/status evidence remains unavailable.
4. The installed MIRcat process-trigger interval, one-command/one-transition
   behavior, `Tuned` reliability, optical settling, scan trajectory, output
   stability, and low/high-rate envelope are unmeasured.
5. Detector transfer, dual normalization, sample/reference latency, timing closure,
   sample-plane optical arrivals, and all configuration-specific IRFs are open.
6. Cell/path, cryostat, 77 K sample-temperature gradient, condensation/icing,
   matrix, beam displacement, heating, mechanical stability, and reset/fresh-position
   equivalence are not qualified.
7. None of the five reconstruction families has passed nonbiological native-coverage,
   missing-data, one-pass coverage, contribution-provenance, deficient-output,
   filter-memory, direction, interpolation, or bias validation.
8. The four mandatory sample-specific initial slow scans have not been acquired;
   therefore final centers, widths, areas, baselines, windows, and sensitivity
   decisions are unknown.
9. Pump dose, probe duty, recovery/reset, cadence, event budget, averaging,
   preparation count, delay/phase grids, scan windows/speeds, and model complexity
   remain condition-specific pilot decisions.
10. The planned HRP phase catalog has no condition-specific 77 K execution package,
    and the MbCO phase catalog does not yet separate room-temperature from 77 K
    execution records. These are downstream experimental-planning gaps, not
    instrument-calibration results.

Until these gaps are resolved through their registered phases, claims are limited to
requirements allocation and cannot assert recipe readiness, measured performance,
biological response, acceptance, completion, or promotion.
