# OM-01 reference-plane and applicability definitions

OM-01 qualifies the available optical power measurement chain. It does not
freeze the final physical coordinates of later optical layouts.

- **Source plane:** immediately after the selected source/output shutter and
  before campaign-retained attenuation or splitting optics.
- **Sample-equivalent plane:** a temporary unobstructed plane reproducing the
  intended sample location without a biological or validation sample present.
- **Sample plane:** the final illuminated specimen plane, established later by
  ATT-01/OG-01.
- **Sample-detector plane:** immediately before the installed sample detector.
- **Reference-detector plane:** immediately before the installed reference
  detector.

Every later reading records one of these names plus a mount/position ID and
layout evidence. OM-01 does not assert equality between planes. Transfer among
them belongs to ATT-01/DET-04/OG-01.

The 2026-08-19 representative acquisition was at a temporary OPO-output
reference plane with both the OPO GUI and meter set to 540 nm and the sensor
acting as the terminal beam stop. The OPO output contained visible halos and
is known not to be spectrally pure. The meter therefore reported combined
incident power under its 540 nm responsivity setting; it did not isolate the
540 nm component. The acquisition qualifies meter operation only and is not a
spectral-purity or sample-plane transfer measurement. PB-02 and the applicable
downstream transfer phase must resolve spectral composition or use qualified
wavelength-selective optics before a wavelength-specific power or dose claim.
