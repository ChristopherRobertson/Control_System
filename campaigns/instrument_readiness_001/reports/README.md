# Instrument-readiness reports

Campaign-level reports assembled from closed phase evidence belong here. A report
does not change a phase status or promote runtime configuration.

Each phase-level thesis narrative remains in its canonical evidence root as
`procedural_writeup.md` and follows
`../../../docs/data_contract/procedural_writeup_standard.md`. Campaign-level thesis
chapters may synthesize accepted phase writeups, but must retain phase/claim/source
traceability and may not broaden their validity envelopes.

`procedural_writeup_backfill_register.md` tracks historical documentation debt
separately from preserved scientific dispositions. Update the register when a
backfill enters review or is accepted; do not change measurement status merely to
reflect document progress.
