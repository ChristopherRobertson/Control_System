# OM-01 polarization applicability

OM-01 makes no absolute polarization measurement or polarization-state claim.
The Newport thermopile is used for total incident average power with the sensor
EEPROM wavelength correction. No polarization correction is introduced in
OM-01.

Polarization-dependent behavior of the OPO, MIRcat, attenuators, splitter,
Mylar orientation, and installed detector arms remains material and is owned by
ATT-01, QB-01, OG-01, SV-01, and DET-04 as applicable. Those phases must record
the source polarization or a bounded observation and may require a polarizer or
other qualified resource. Absence of a polarimeter does not block the narrower
OM-01 meter-readiness claim, but it limits downstream polarization claims.
