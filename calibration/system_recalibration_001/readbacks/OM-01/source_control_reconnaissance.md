# OM-01 source-control reconnaissance

## Nd:YAG / OPO delay assessment

Operator-reported prior assessment; acquisition date and raw samples were not
provided.

- Observed wavelength: 632 nm OPO output.
- Control: delay between FIRE and Q-SWITCH commands from the digital delay
  generators.
- Assessed interval: 200--300 us.
- Approximate maximum: 245 us, about 75 mW average indicated power.
- Approximate 300 us result: less than 35 mW.
- Observation method: no power-meter filter; operator estimated the midpoint
  between the highest and lowest displayed values over approximately 10 s.

Classification: useful reconnaissance for locating settings and choosing a
safe meter range, but not accepted quantitative calibration evidence. It does
not need to be repeated unless 632 nm or the delay-response curve is retained
in a thesis claim. If repeated, use automated status-aware sampling and retain
the full time series.

## MIRcat current control

Operator reports that output is controlled by current and that lower-current
operation has larger and more frequent power fluctuations. OM-01 therefore
reports time-series variability at every retained low-current operating point
and does not assume a linear current-to-power relationship. Low-current points
not used for a retained claim are not added solely for meter qualification.
