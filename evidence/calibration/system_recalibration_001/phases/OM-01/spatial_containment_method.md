# OM-01 binary spatial-containment method

The 919P-010-16 active absorber diameter is 16 mm (8 mm radius). For OM-01
total-power readiness, the qualified alternative to a beam profiler is a binary
aperture-containment observation:

1. Mount the sensor approximately normal to the unfocused beam at the stated
   reference plane.
2. Center the beam on the absorber.
3. Confirm that the entire visible beam footprint falls within the active
   sensor radius, with no observed clipping at the absorber boundary.
4. Record wavelength, plane, mount state, and operator observation.

On 2026-08-19, Christopher Robertson confirmed that the entire visible OPO
footprint fit within the sensor radius during the accepted representative
acquisition. This confirmation includes the visible halos around the central
beam that may contain unwanted wavelengths and/or spatial structure. The
sensor was the terminal beam stop and no clipping was observed.

This is a binary containment result only. It does not report beam diameter,
Gaussian radius, ellipticity, divergence, fluence, or a numerical edge margin.
OG-01 must establish quantitative beam geometry at final retained planes, and
invisible 355 nm/mid-IR conditions require an appropriate card, camera,
knife-edge, or other separately qualified method rather than unaided viewing.
This observation establishes spatial capture only and does not establish the
spectral identity or purity of the captured radiation.
